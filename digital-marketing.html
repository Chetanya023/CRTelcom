        Campaigns run by the rules.

        Voice calls, SMS and lead forms are all regulated in India. We build the rules in so a good campaign doesn't get blocked or penalised.

        Promotional calls use 140

        Automated promotional calls go out on 140-series numbers. Service and transactional calls use a separate series, and regulated entities like banks and insurers are moving those to 1600.

        Consent on record

        Promotional calls and messages need recorded consent, and DND preferences must be respected. Consent given for one purpose doesn't cover everything, and some consent expires, so we log when and how it was given.

        Declare and schedule

        Auto-dialers are declared to your telecom operator before launch, and calls run only within permitted hours. We configure the dialer so those limits are enforced by the system.

        Forms that respect data law

        India's data protection rules, notified in November 2025 and phasing in through May 2027, expect a clear notice and specific consent. Our forms state what you collect and why, and store the consent.

        Rules change and operators interpret them differently. Treat this as a guide and confirm current requirements with your operator and legal adviser. Checked October 2026.

          How long does SEO take to work?

          Usually months, not weeks. Technical fixes and local listings can show movement earlier, while competitive searches take longer to build. We set targets in the first month and report monthly on enquiries as well as rankings.

          Do you guarantee rankings or leads?

          No, and be careful with anyone who does. Search engines and ad platforms are outside anyone's control. We commit to the work, the tracking and honest reporting, and we agree measurable targets with you.

          What is the difference between OBD and IVR?

          OBD (outbound dialling) is the engine that places calls to a list. IVR is the menu that runs once someone answers, such as "press 1 to talk to us". Most marketing campaigns use both: the OBD calls, and the IVR sorts the interested from the rest.

          Are OBD marketing calls legal in India?

          Yes, within TRAI's rules. Promotional automated calls must use 140-series numbers, go to people who have consented and aren't blocking such calls, and the auto-dialer use must be declared to your operator in advance. Calls must also stay within permitted hours. We configure all of this with you.

          Who owns the website, domain and ad accounts?

          You do. Domain, hosting, analytics and ad accounts are registered in your name, and we work inside them, so you can take everything with you.

          Can leads from ads get an instant WhatsApp and call?

          Yes. Digital Marketing connects to the same platform as our Products. A new lead can get a WhatsApp message in seconds, an IVR call if they haven't replied, and an SMS after that, all logged against the lead.
